About Sabor

A family story told one dish at a time, since 2013.

How it started

From six tables to the neighbourhood's living room

Sabor opened in 2013 with six tables, a second-hand espresso machine and recipes from our grandmother's notebook. The idea was simple: cook what the market gives you that morning, treat every guest like a neighbour, and never rush anyone out of their chair.

Today we work with eleven local farmers, bake all our bread and pastries in-house, and roast our own coffee blend in small weekly batches. The menu changes with the seasons because the market does — and because food tastes better when it didn't travel far.
