Our story

A small dining room with a big heart

Sabor began as a family café with six tables and one stubborn idea: food should be honest, seasonal and shared. Twelve years later the dining room is bigger, but the idea hasn't changed.

We buy from local farmers every morning, bake our bread in-house and roast our own coffee blend — so everything on your table travelled less than a day to reach it.
